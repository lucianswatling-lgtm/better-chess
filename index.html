TactiCore Codex: Units & Cataclysms

Victory Condition: Capture the enemy Sovereign (Core King) or leave your opponent with no legal moves! Unlike normal chess, every piece has upgraded tactical abilities and the board mutates with cosmic events.

⚡ Custom Tactical Units

🛡️ Vanguard (Upgraded Pawn)

Moves 1 step forward OR sideways (2 steps forward on start row). Captures 1 step diagonally forward. Promotes to Archon at the far edge.

🏛️ Bastion (Juggernaut Rook)

Slides horizontally & vertically. Meteor Proof: Falling meteors cannot destroy a Bastion, and it can smash through Crystal Spikes!

🐎 Phase Strider (Agile Leaper)

Jumps in an L-shape (like a Knight) OR leaps exactly 2 tiles orthogonally over obstacles.

🔮 Warp Mage (Diagonal Teleporter)

Slides diagonally. Special Ability: Can warp-swap positions with any friendly unit within 2 tiles!

👸 Archon (Star Queen)

Crowned with a floating Star Halo! Slides up to 3 tiles in any direction AND can make L-shape knight jumps.

🤴 Sovereign (Armored Monarch)

Humanoid Commander with a golden visor & royal crown! Steps 1 tile any way or dashes 2 tiles orthogonally.

☄️ Dynamic Arena Cataclysms & Modes

✈️ Bombing Jet Airstrike

Strike fighters scream across the board and carpet-bomb a tactical run, destroying exposed pieces (except Sovereigns & Bastions)!

🧟 Zombie Infection Mode

Turns the arena blood-red! Whenever you capture an enemy unit, it mutates into your own team's unit on your back rank!

☄️ Meteor Impact

Strikes a random tile! Vaporizes any piece standing there (except Bastions & Sovereigns) and leaves a scorched crater for several turns.

💎 Crystal Spike

Erupts from the ground, blocking movement onto or past that tile until it shatters a few turns later.

❄️ Cryo Freeze

Freezes a random unit in solid ice for 2 turns—it cannot move or be captured until thawed!

🪂 45s Reinforcements

Lost a unit? It enters the Reinforce Pod queue and automatically drops back onto your side after 45 seconds!
